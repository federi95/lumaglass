# Screenshots and media

## Lumaglass feature highlights

The [current feature gallery](highlights/README.md) contains 12 native Lumaglass
screenshots from CI: Home/dock, apps, Control Center, profile, settings, wallpaper
gallery, glass on/off and photo sources. They use desktop rendering and demo data;
they do not establish TV hardware compatibility. Capture provenance and asset
attributions accompany the gallery.

## Upstream Luma media

Screenshots, contact sheet and `luma-preview.mp4` were inherited from Luma at the
fork baseline. Their pixels retain upstream branding and illustrate the original
native design, not newly captured Lumaglass behavior or TV hardware validation.

Original project: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma

They have been moved without alteration. Source artwork and visible third-party
logos retain the terms in [../assets.md](../assets.md). New Lumaglass captures live separately under `highlights/` and identify their
source revision, CI environment and demonstration data.
