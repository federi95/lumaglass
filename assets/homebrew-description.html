Lumaglass is an independent native fork of Luma, with planned TV-management features inspired by Glasshouse.

A native launcher for rooted LG webOS TVs, inspired by Apple TV.

Launch apps and inputs, organize your favorites, and browse your app library with remote or Magic Remote pointer controls. Customize backgrounds and input labels, and add weather for a city of your choice. English, German, Spanish and Simplified Chinese are included.

Requires a rooted TV and elevated Homebrew Channel.
